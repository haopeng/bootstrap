MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Brightline Analytics LLC ("Client")
and Quantum Data Services, Inc. ("Vendor"). Each a "Party".

1. Term. The initial term is twelve (12) months. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive
twelve (12) month periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90)
days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2. Fees. Client shall pay all invoices Net 30. A late fee of 1.5% per month applies to overdue amounts.
Vendor reserves the right to modify pricing at any time without notice.

3. Indemnification. Client shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Vendor from any and all claims,
losses, damages and expenses arising out of or related to Client's use of the Services.

4. Non-Competition. During the term and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Client shall not engage
any competing data services provider.

5. Assignment. Client may not assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of Vendor.

6. Confidentiality. Each Party shall keep Confidential Information confidential for a period of 5 years.

7. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Parties hereby execute this Agreement.
